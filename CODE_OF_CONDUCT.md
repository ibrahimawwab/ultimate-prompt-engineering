# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive learning community. Participation is expected to be professional and considerate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when reviewing ideas and correcting mistakes.
- Critique content, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and relevant context.
- Welcome good-faith questions and beginner contributions.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized harassment, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Spam and knowingly deceptive or malicious contributions may be removed.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and communicated where practical.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

This code applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
